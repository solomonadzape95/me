## why i built it

part of my work at [accave](https://accave.com) involved turning recorded lessons into notes and assessments. jotter started as a way to remove the repetitive first pass.

## how it works

you drop in a recording, jotter transcribes it, then generates a set of notes and questions. both open in an in-page editor for review before the files are named and downloaded.

## next

i would like it to pull recordings directly from microsoft teams so the full workflow can happen without manual uploads.
