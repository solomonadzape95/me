## about
This is the original football edition of Gauntlet: a last-player-standing pool where players back a footballer each round and continue until one player remains.

## current status
The football build is preserved as a legacy project on Sui. Its interface demonstrates wallet connection, a SUI entry pool, the pick flow, and player-facing FAQs; it is separate from the newer Base fantasy-stock edition and does not claim a production audit or production readiness.
