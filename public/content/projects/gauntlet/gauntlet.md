## about
Gauntlet is a last-player-standing pool for football. Players make their picks round by round, and the pool continues until one player remains.

## current status
Gauntlet is an in-progress Season 0 product on Sui. The published interface shows wallet connection, a SUI entry pool, the pick flow, and player-facing FAQs; this case study does not claim a production audit or production readiness.
